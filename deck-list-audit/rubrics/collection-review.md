# Collection review rubric

Use for lineup comparisons, overlap, social fit, and portfolio decisions.

- Require every compared deck to pass deterministic validation against the
  same Oracle and policy snapshots.
- Compare power, speed, interaction, commander dependence, replayability,
  threat perception, salt drivers, good-stuff drift, distinctive gameplay,
  known sharp lines, and Rule Zero dependencies.
- Distinguish raw power from blind-pod safety and from uniqueness within the
  collection.
- Do not transfer a finding from an earlier list state. Tie every conclusion to
  the deck SHA-256 and evidence dates.
- State whether ordering is objective, evidence-supported judgment, or personal
  preference. Preserve unselected names or deck replacements as proposals.
