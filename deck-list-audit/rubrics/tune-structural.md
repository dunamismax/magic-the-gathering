# Structural tuning rubric

Use only when the user asks for a broad rebuild, strongest practical package,
or correction of structural problems.

- Audit the complete deck as a system: curve, mana, ramp, card flow,
  interaction, protection, consistency, threat density, closing power,
  resilience, commander synergy, and weak subthemes.
- Preserve hard constraints and intended identity unless the user explicitly
  authorizes changing them.
- Use one cohesive equal Adds/Cuts package. It may rebalance roles rather than
  use direct one-for-one replacements.
- Make as many changes as materially justified, but do not pad the package.
- Quantify the before/after structural effects and rerun hard validation,
  Game Changer checks, and combo/social screens on the resulting 100.
- Keep the actionable list Moxfield-ready and label it proposed until applied.
