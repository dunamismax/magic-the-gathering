Reviewed: 2026-09-08
Scope: all 11 exact public Commander lists in the [September 8 source manifest](../data/moxfield-exports/2026-09-08/manifest.json).

Smaug occupies the highest-power intentional role in this lineup. Its six Game Changers fit the unrestricted B4 ceiling, while compact combos, tutor access, acceleration, and protection support that placement independently of the count. The seven-mana commander imposes a real setup cost and concentrated vulnerability to exile, bounce, sacrifice, and ability suppression. The list may win on the turn Smaug resolves; an exact typical or earliest win turn was not measured. There is no basis here to certify cEDH performance or to claim that every card slot is mathematically optimal.

The existing-deck judgments are retained against unchanged hashes, with medium confidence. These descriptions are pregame guidance, not measured rankings. Minn's two-extra-turn ultimate and Gandalf's alternate unlimited-combat finish still need explicit discussion. Smaug should be introduced as the optimized combo option before choosing it for a pod.

All 11 mains resolve to 100 cards and pass configured checks. Smaug has 35 printed lands plus Valakut Awakening as one spell/land MDFC, no banned cards, and no Rule Zero dependency. The existing dependencies remain: Blor and Maular release November 9; The Theorist, Jace Beleren and Darksteel Angel release October 2; Gandalf's Maximum Flavor main includes off-identity Westward Voyager; Frodo & Sam needs The One Ring bracket exception for B2*.

Smaug's nine included Spellbook candidates were reviewed individually, including finite effects and the erroneous Dawnsire/Fiendlash damage description; Blazing Sunsteel was manually confirmed as an additional repeatable engine. Full prerequisites, payoff coverage, the public primer's three absent-card references, existing-deck combo disclosures, and source dates are in the [social audit](commander-social-audit-2026-09-08.md). The ten existing scans retain their actual dates; only Smaug needed a new scan. Combo completeness and real game speed remain uncertain.

`reports/manifest.json` pins both reports and all 11 current deck hashes. The historical September 5 report retains its old numerical estimates as dated judgments; this update uses bracket and play-pattern descriptions instead of adding an unmeasured numerical score for Smaug.
