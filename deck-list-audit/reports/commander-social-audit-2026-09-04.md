## Changed and new deck findings

### The Notary Hobbits

The commander creates two nonlegendary copies. Once the trio can tap, each sees
three Halflings and produces three colorless mana. A haste effect therefore
turns the baseline commander into nine immediate mana; Parallel Lives or
Panharmonicon produces five Notaries and raises the ceiling to 25. Changelings,
Maskwood Nexus, and Springleaf Parade scale the engine before the deck converts
mana into Eldrazi cast triggers and combat.

The list has no complete infinite loop. Thousand-Year Elixir supplies one
untap, while the token and trigger multipliers are finite. Its high-end B3
placement comes from explosive command-zone mana, Finale of Devastation,
premium lands, Eldrazi cast triggers, annihilator, and Rise of the Eldrazi.
Darksteel Angel is an unreleased preview and needs a Rule Zero conversation
until 2026-10-02.

### Gandalf, Party Guest

**Maximum Flavor — Bracket 2-style Rule Zero.** The posted main deck contains
all eleven printed Gandalfs plus Gandalf's Sanction. It removes the dedicated
tutor, extra-turn spell, artifact trigger multipliers, fast Mox, and
unlimited-combat package. It has zero Game Changers and no complete combo, but
Gandalf, Westward Voyager requires an explicit color-identity house rule.

**Maximum Power — Bracket 3.** Remove Gandalf of the Secret Fire; Gandalf the
White; Gandalf, Friend of the Shire; Gandalf, Shadow's Foe; Gandalf, Spark
Starter; Gandalf, Wandering Wizard; Gandalf, Westward Voyager; Gandalf, White
Rider; and Gandalf's Sanction. Add the complete sideboard: Mizzix of the
Izmagnus; Mox Amber; Roaming Throne; Savage Beating; Solve the Equation; Surge
to Victory; The Emperor of Palamecia; Time Warp; and Wizard's Staff. No other
cards change.

That streamlined, maximally tuned configuration keeps only the strongest
Gandalfs and restores acceleration, trigger multiplication, tutoring, an extra
turn, and the deck's intentional conditional unlimited-combat finish. The line
requires Savage Beating in the graveyard and a creature that can continue
connecting; disclose it when using Maximum Power. Reverse the same exact
nine-for-nine exchange to return to Maximum Flavor.

### Frodo & Sam

The One Ring replaces Shire Terrace. This keeps the exact list at 100 cards and
reduces printed lands from 37 to 36, while adding the collection's only current
Game Changer. It does not complete a Food, life-gain, sacrifice, or historic
recursion loop. The normal plan remains incremental and board-dependent, but
the public B2* label now needs an explicit Game Changer exception; present it as
B3 if the table follows the ordinary bracket framework.

### Minn, Wily Illusionist

Living Lies of Loki, Lyla, Holographic Assistant, and Monument to Endurance
replace Molecule Man, Oneirophage, and Psychosis Crawler. The new cards improve
Illusion density, death-trigger draw, counter scaling, and discard value. They
do not return sacrificed Illusions or create an unrestricted loop with Minn's
once-per-turn second-draw trigger, so the exact-list combo result remains none.
