## Verdict

configurations. Gandalf's Maximum Flavor main is Bracket 2-style with a Rule
Zero color-identity exception; its exact nine-for-nine switch creates the
streamlined, maximally tuned, Commander-legal B3 Maximum Power mode. Frodo & Sam
plays like upper B2 but contains The One Ring, so its
B2* label depends on a pregame bracket exception and otherwise should be B3.

## Main changes

The Notary Hobbits is a high-end B3 because the commander alone becomes three
mana creatures that can produce nine mana, and finite token multipliers can
raise that burst to 25. The list turns that mana into Eldrazi cast triggers,
annihilator, Finale of Devastation, and Rise of the Eldrazi. It has no intended
or detected infinite combo, dense fast mana, or compact instant win.

Gandalf's posted Maximum Flavor main moved down from the prior ceiling-B3 power
list. Its nine power cards are now in a sideboard, and the main contains all
eleven printed Gandalfs plus Gandalf's Sanction. It has no access to the
two-card combat loop. The exact nine-for-nine Maximum Power switch retains only
the strongest Gandalfs and restores acceleration, trigger multiplication,
tutoring, an extra turn, and the intentional conditional unlimited-combat
finish. No other cards change. Maximum Power remains B3 and must disclose that
line.

Frodo & Sam adds The One Ring. Its creature, Food, life-gain, and combat plan
still matches upper-B2 pacing, but The One Ring is on the current Game Changers
list. Bracket 3 is the clean label when a pod does not agree to the displayed
B2* exception.

Minn's three-for-three update improves synergy without adding a deterministic
line or a faster close. Middle B3 remains appropriate.

## Collection-wide facts and caveats

- All 10 exact main lists contain 100 cards and pass the configured audit.
- The lineup contains one Game Changer: The One Ring in Frodo & Sam.
- No list contains a mass-land-denial package.
- Pantlaza has three conditional near-infinite lines. Queen Marchesa has one
  finite kill and one five-card unbounded loop. Henzie's returned persist
  template is incomplete. Gandalf's conditional unlimited-combat line exists
  only in its Maximum Power configuration.
- Rule Zero is currently required for Blor, Gandalf's Westward Voyager house
  rule, Minn's The Theorist, Jace Beleren, Pantlaza's Maular, and Notary's
  Darksteel Angel.

## Evidence and limits

This evaluation uses the hashes in `data/moxfield-refresh.json`, the 2026-09-04
Oracle snapshot, official Commander policy pages checked on 2026-09-04,
hash-current Spellbook evidence, and the manual review in
`knowledge/combo-adjudications.json`. Brackets remain a pregame communication
tool, and a database miss does not prove the absence of every possible combo.
