## Verdict

deck. Gandalf's Maximum Flavor main plays as a B2-style Rule Zero configuration
despite its posted B3 label; its exact nine-for-nine switch creates the
streamlined B3 Maximum Power mode. Frodo & Sam plays like upper B2 but contains
The One Ring, so its B2* label depends on a pregame bracket exception and
otherwise should be B3.

## Thorin assessment

Thorin brings an all-Dwarf Treasure identity in a Boros shell. The commander rewards every Dwarf ETB with a
Treasure, then converts artifact tokens into a scaling power boost. Thirty-one
Dwarves, Dwarven Recruiter, Magda inside the 99, trigger and token multipliers,
free protection, and combat closers give the deck high synergy and strong
recovery. It has 35 printed lands plus two spell/land MDFCs and zero Game
Changers.

Commander Spellbook returned no complete exact-list combo. Manual review found
finite multiplication only: there is no repeatable untap engine for Dwarven
Bloodboiler and no Aggravated Assault, Hellkite Charger, or Clock of Omens for
the nearby Treasure and extra-combat templates. Upper B3, around 7.5-8.0/10, is
the best current fit.

## Retained-list refreshes

Henzie's current 12-for-12 update trades Sol Ring, two one-mana Elves, Veil of
Summer, and slower lands for premium fixing, graveyard recursion, and Kardur's
combat control. It remains mid-to-upper B3, and its Mikaeus/Viscera template
still lacks a persist creature. Frodo & Sam replaces Terramorphic Expanse with
Hobbit Hole; that thematic land change does not alter its construction or
bracket findings.

## Collection-wide facts and caveats

- All 10 exact main lists contain 100 cards and pass the configured audit.
- The lineup contains one Game Changer: The One Ring in Frodo & Sam.
- No list contains a mass-land-denial package.
- Pantlaza has three conditional near-infinite lines. Queen Marchesa has one
  finite kill and one five-card unbounded loop. Henzie's returned persist
  template is incomplete. Gandalf's conditional unlimited-combat line exists
  only in its Maximum Power configuration.
- Rule Zero is currently required for Blor, Gandalf's Westward Voyager house
  rule, Minn's The Theorist, Jace Beleren, Pantlaza's Maular, and Notary's
  Darksteel Angel.

## Evidence and limits

This evaluation uses the hashes in `data/moxfield-refresh.json`, the 2026-09-04
Oracle snapshot, official Commander policy pages checked on 2026-09-04,
hash-current Spellbook evidence, and the manual review in
`knowledge/combo-adjudications.json`. Brackets remain a pregame communication
tool, and a database miss does not prove the absence of every possible combo.
